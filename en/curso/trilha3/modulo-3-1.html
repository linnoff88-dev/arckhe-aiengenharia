ê°Å¸Å½Â¨ Brand identity and voice as reusable context

The context you build once and use all the time

Without voice context, every response sounds like it came from a different company. With it, even someone who just opened the tool can produce something in the right toneê¢â¬âbecause the context carries the consistency standard.

1
Define the tone in 3 adjectives

E.g.: "direct, empathetic, technical without jargon." Simple and concrete ê¢â¬â AI knows what to do with that.

2
List what to avoid

E.g.: "never use the word 'synergy,' don't promise financial results, avoid humor when the topic is a customer's problems."

3
Add 1-2 real examples

Copy an excerpt from a text of yours that you consider to have the right tone. That's the most powerful style anchor.

4
Paste this at the start of every new conversation

Save it as an "identity block" somewhere easy to access. 30 seconds of copy and paste ensures consistency throughout the session.
